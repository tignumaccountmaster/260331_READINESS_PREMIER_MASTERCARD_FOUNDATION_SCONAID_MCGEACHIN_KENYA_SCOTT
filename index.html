Reading it

What this group's 1.13 actually means

This is a genuinely strong cohort, clearly above the benchmark. A mean beta of 1.13 translates to roughly 60% on the readiness scale — upper-Moderate, and above the average of every leader measured (1.00). On the performance-impact framing, this group's readiness is actively multiplying rather than merely breaking even.

The profile is unusually uniform and healthy: seven of eight leaders sit in Moderate on every level, none in distress, and the group scores above the database on all three self-rated states — including carrying less fear about future demands than typical. The clearest development opportunity is the same soft pillar seen everywhere — Nutrition, especially pre-event fuelling — but here it's a matter of pushing a strong group from Moderate into High, not repairing a gap.

One caution on the headline: the single 1.13 figure is pulled down by one low outlier (Participant 314280, 0.81). Strip that leader and the rest cluster tightly above 1.13. The outlier deserves individual attention; the other seven are already performing at or above where you'd want the group to be. Averages describe the group; they don't describe anyone in it.
